Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Short version

  • WalletTrail has no server. The developer does not receive your data.
  • Your data is on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is also in your own iCloud account.
  • The app has no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.
  • The developer does not sell your data.
  • A shared ledger is visible to the people you invite. It is stored in the iCloud account of the ledger owner.

1. Who we are

WalletTrail is made by Ajith K James. You can contact us at wallettrail.support@icloud.com.

2. What data the app keeps

You enter all data yourself. The app does not connect to banks, brokers, or other services. It does not read your SMS messages, emails, or Apple Wallet.

The app keeps the data that you enter, for example:

  • Ledgers, members, and member names.
  • Transactions, categories, accounts, tags, budgets, and notes.
  • Holdings, values, and exchange rates that you type.
  • Your settings, for example the app lock, number format, and currency.

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your iPhone. All data is stored on your device. The app works with no network connection.
  • In your iCloud account. If you turn on iCloud sync, the app copies your data to your own iCloud account with Apple's iCloud service. This keeps your devices up to date. Apple stores this data, under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see it.
  • In shared ledgers. See section 4.

You can turn iCloud sync off in Settings. Then your private ledgers stay on your iPhone only.

4. Shared ledgers

  • You can share a ledger with other people through iCloud.
  • Everyone you invite can see all data in that ledger. They can change it if you give them permission to edit.
  • A shared ledger is stored in the iCloud account of its owner. It uses the owner's iCloud storage.
  • Each record in a shared ledger shows who added it and who changed it last, with the time.
  • Each person sees only the ledgers that are shared with them. Your other ledgers stay private.
  • The owner can remove a person or stop sharing at any time. A person can leave a shared ledger at any time. Their entries stay in the ledger.
  • Apple's iCloud sharing can show the name, email address, or phone number of each person in a share to the other people in it. This information comes from Apple, not from the app.

5. What the developer receives

  • The developer does not receive the data that you enter in the app.
  • The app has no analytics and no third-party software that collects data.
  • The app does not track you, and it does not show ads.
  • The developer does not sell, rent, or share your data.
  • Crash reports and app statistics. If you agree in your iPhone settings to share data with app developers, Apple can give the developer crash reports and general statistics about the app. These come from Apple's tools (Xcode Organizer, MetricKit, and App Store Connect). They do not contain the data that you enter in the app.
  • Beta feedback. If you test a beta version with TestFlight and send feedback, Apple gives the developer your feedback, any screenshot you attach, and basic device information. We use it only to improve the app.
  • Support emails. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you. We delete support emails when we no longer need them to answer you, and at the latest after 12 months.

6. Security

  • The app stores its data with iOS Data Protection.
  • You can turn on an app lock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. iOS checks your face, fingerprint, or passcode. The app does not receive this data.
  • The app can hide its content in the app switcher.
  • The app does not write your financial data to its logs.

7. Export, backup, and deletion

  • You can export your transactions and holdings as CSV files, and back up your ledgers to a file. You choose where these files go.
  • You can delete a ledger that you own. It is deleted for all members.
  • You can leave a ledger that someone else shares with you.
  • "Delete all my data" in Settings deletes all the ledgers that you own and leaves the ledgers that you joined.
  • If you delete the app, your data in iCloud is not deleted. You can remove it in the iPhone Settings app, under your Apple Account and iCloud.

8. Children

The app is not made for children. The developer does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

9. Your rights

You have control of your data in the app: you can see, export, change, and delete it at any time. The developer holds no copy of it. If you emailed us, you can ask us to show or delete your emails. If you have a question about your privacy rights, contact us at wallettrail.support@icloud.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
