Support

Questions, problems, or ideas: email wallettrail.support@icloud.com.

A support email is personal data. We use it only to answer you. See the Privacy Policy.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, also in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot see it.

How do I back up my data? In the app, open Settings, then Data. You can export CSV files and make a backup file.

How do I delete my data? In the app, open Settings, then Data, then “Delete All My Data”.

Beta testers: send feedback from the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot in WalletTrail to send feedback.
