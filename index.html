WalletTrail

A private money tracker for iPhone: expenses, budgets, and net worth.

Now in beta testing with TestFlight. Coming to the App Store.

Private by design

No server, no login, no bank links, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Share a ledger

Track shared costs with a partner or friends through iCloud. Splits and balances always add up exactly.

Budgets and net worth

Plan monthly budgets, follow your assets and debts, and see facts about where your money goes.

Works offline

Every feature works with no network connection. You enter all data yourself.
