Questions about WalletTrail

Does WalletTrail connect to my bank?

No. You enter each expense, income and value yourself. The app never fetches bank data, prices or exchange rates.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud account when sync is on. A shared ledger lives in iCloud for the people you invited.

Can I use it without iCloud?

Yes. Turn sync off and everything stays on this iPhone. You can export your data at any time.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The beta runs through TestFlight, Apple's app for test builds.

Does it give financial advice?

No. Insights state facts about your entries, such as which category went up. Decisions stay with you.

How do I delete my data?

Delete All My Data in Settings deletes every ledger you own. Ledgers that others shared with you stay with their owners.
